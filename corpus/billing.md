# Plans and billing

## Plans

Larkspur has three plans. Free supports up to 5 members and 3 projects. Team costs $8 per member per month and removes the project limit. Business costs $15 per member per month and adds single sign-on, audit logs, and a 99.9% uptime commitment.

## How seats are counted

You pay for each active member. Guests who can only view and comment are free. When you add a member in the middle of a billing cycle, the charge is prorated to the day.

## Changing plans

Owners can upgrade at any time and the change applies immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. If a workspace on Free goes over 3 projects after a downgrade, the extra projects become read only until some are archived.

## Invoices and refunds

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and can be downloaded under Settings, Billing. To change the company name or address on future invoices, edit the billing profile on the same page. Duplicate charges are refunded in full within 5 business days. Other refunds are reviewed case by case.

## Annual billing

Annual plans are billed once a year at a 20% discount compared with monthly billing.
