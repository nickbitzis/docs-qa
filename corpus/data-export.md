# Exporting your data

## Exporting a project

Open a project, select the menu, and choose Export. You can export to CSV or JSON. CSV exports include tasks, assignees, due dates, and labels. JSON exports also include comments, subtasks, and the full activity history.

## Full workspace export

Owners can export the whole workspace under Settings, Data. The export runs in the background and you get an email with a download link when it is ready. Links expire after 48 hours. Large workspaces can take up to an hour to export.

## Attachments

File attachments are not included in CSV exports. JSON exports include a signed link for each attachment that stays valid for 7 days.

## Known issues

Very large boards with more than 10,000 tasks may time out when exported to CSV from the project menu. Use the full workspace export for these boards instead.
