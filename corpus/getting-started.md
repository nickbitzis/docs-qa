# Getting started with Larkspur

Larkspur is a task tracker for small teams. This guide covers the first ten minutes.

## Create a workspace

Sign up at the Larkspur website and choose a workspace name. The name appears in your workspace URL and can be changed later under Settings, General. Each workspace has its own members, projects, and billing.

## Invite your team

Open Settings, Members, and select Invite. Enter one email address per line. Invitations expire after 7 days. Owners and admins can resend an expired invitation from the same page.

## Roles

There are three roles. Owners manage billing and can delete the workspace. Admins manage members, projects, and integrations. Members create and edit tasks in projects they belong to. A workspace must always have at least one owner.

## Your first project

Select New project and pick a template or start blank. Projects hold tasks, and tasks can have an assignee, a due date, labels, and subtasks up to three levels deep.
